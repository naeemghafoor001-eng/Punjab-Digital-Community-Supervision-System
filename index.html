System Governance & Technical Safeguards

Fictional records only at this stage — All data shown in this prototype is synthetic and for demonstration purposes.
No live government connections — Not connected to any official PP&PS, court, prison, police, NADRA or government identity database.
Non-intrusive supervision — No GPS, camera or biometric collection in current version.
Human-in-the-loop review — Alerts are for officer review only and are not automatic violation findings.
Production Deployment Protocol — Real data testing will require login, role-based access and approved security controls.
