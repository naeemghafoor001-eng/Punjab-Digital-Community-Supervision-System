Project Scope

  • Modernizing community supervision for non-custodial probationers and parolees across Punjab.
  • Promoting structured rehabilitation, vocational training enrollment, and reduced recidivism.
  • Providing transparent district-level compliance monitoring for officers and executive management.

Demonstration Limitations

  • All workflows are non-operational demonstration models using 100% fictional data.
  • No real probationer, parolee, officer, CNIC, or court data is stored or processed.
  • No device sensors, camera permissions, GPS location tracking, or biometrics are accessed.
  • No live backend API connections or official government database integrations exist.

Privacy Principles

  • Strict adherence to data minimization and non-intrusive community supervision standards.
  • Demonstration identity numbers are synthetic and fully masked (e.g. 00000-0000000-0).
  • Designed with ethical technology practices prioritizing user privacy and dignity.
