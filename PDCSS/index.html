System Governance & Technical Standards

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) — Strict credentialed access scoping across Supervisee, Officer, and Directorate General tiers.
Mandatory Officer Review — Automated alerts and submissions require officer verification before compliance determination.
Single-Point Verification — Location and attendance verification operate strictly at point-of-submission without continuous monitoring.
Data Privacy & Protection — Offender records are protected with data masking and strict access control boundaries.
