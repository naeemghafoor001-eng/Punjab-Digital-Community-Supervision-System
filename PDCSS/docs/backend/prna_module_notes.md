# Punjab Risk & Needs Assessment (PRNA) & Case Planning Module Notes

**Department**: Punjab Probation and Parole Service  
**Parent Entity**: Home Department, Government of the Punjab  
**System**: Raahnuma — Punjab Community Supervision System  

---

## 1. Overview & Operational Purpose

The **Punjab Risk & Needs Assessment (PRNA) — Adult** module establishes a standardized, evidence-based assessment and RNR-aligned (Risk-Need-Responsivity) case planning framework for probation and parole officers across Punjab.

### Core Objectives:
1. **Intake Screening & Timeline**: Every probationer and parolee placed under community supervision must have an initial PRNA assessment conducted within the **first 30 days of placement**.
2. **Reassessment Lifecycle**: Reassessments must be performed:
   - **90 days** post-initial assessment to gauge early intervention responsiveness.
   - **Upon major life events** (e.g., job change, housing transition, breach warning, family crisis).
   - **Every 6 months** during the active supervision term.
3. **Decision Support & Case Management**: Scores directly inform supervision intensity allocation and criminogenic target selection for case planning.
4. **No Automated Sanctions**: The module serves strictly to support officer decision-making and rehabilitation planning. It **must not** produce automatic legal consequences or automatic violation findings.

---

## 2. Assessment Architecture & Scoring Formula

The PRNA tool comprises 9 sections:

| Section | Title | Scoring Range | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Section A** | Administrative Information | *Not Scored* | Case reference, CNIC mask, court order dates, placement dates. |
| **Section B** | Static Risk Index (SRI) | 0 – 24 | Historical offense factors (prior convictions, age at first conviction, violent history, prior supervision failure). |
| **Section C** | Dynamic Needs Index (DNI) | 0 – 36 | 7 criminogenic domains (Education/Employment, Family, Peers, Substance Use, Cognition, Leisure, Housing). |
| **Section D** | Personality & Conflict Response (PCR) | 0 – 16 | Situational judgement and officer observational checklist for responsivity. |
| **Section E** | Protective Factors Index (PFI) | 0 to -8 | Pro-social strengths (stable job offer, education, pro-social mentor, community engagement, stable housing). |
| **Section F** | Scoring Worksheet | Calculated | Combined total risk & needs score calculation. |
| **Section G** | Supervision Intensity Matrix | Classification | Maps total score to supervision intensity band. |
| **Section H** | RNR-Aligned Case Plan | Case Plan | SMART goals, interventions, steps, and action links to assigned activities. |
| **Section I** | Declarations & Review | Sign-off | Assessor declaration and supervisor approval. |

### Scoring Calculation Formula:
$$\text{TOTAL RISK SCORE} = \text{SRI} + \text{DNI} + \text{PCR} - \text{PFI}$$

### Provisional Risk Band Classifications:

| Total Score | Risk Band | Supervision Intensity Level |
|---|---|---|
| **$\le$ 16** | **Low** | Low Intensity (Monthly Reporting / Self-Management) |
| **17 – 26** | **Moderate** | Standard Supervision (Bi-Weekly Reporting) |
| **27 – 34** | **High** | Intensive Oversight (Weekly Reporting & Home Visits) |
| **$\ge$ 35** | **Very High** | High-Oversight Specialized Rehabilitation |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Provisional Validation Note**: All risk bands generated by the PRNA tool are provisional and subject to formal Punjab empirical validation and officer discretion.

## 3. Case Plan Linkage to Assigned Activities

To operationalize the RNR model, Case Plan Action items directly link to **Assigned Activities** inside the Supervisee App:

- **Employment / Skills Need** $\rightarrow$ Linked to *Skills Training / TEVTA Attendance*
- **Counselling Need** $\rightarrow$ Linked to *Counselling Session Attendance*
- **Health / Wellness Need** $\rightarrow$ Linked to *Exercise / Health Activity*
- **Community Engagement** $\rightarrow$ Linked to *Community Service Duty*
- **Personal Discipline** $\rightarrow$ Linked to *Spiritual / Personal Discipline Activity* (Voluntary & Lawful)

---

## 4. Privacy & Data Visibility Controls

- **Officer App & Web Portal**: Complete access to detailed section scores, item responses, risk bands, and officer notes (English-only).
- **Supervisee App**: Displays **only** pro-social supervision goals, assigned activities, rehabilitation referrals, next review date, officer instructions, and progress status.
- **Strict Prohibition**: Supervisee App **never** displays SRI, DNI, PCR, PFI, Total scores, or risk band labels (Low/Moderate/High/Very High) to prevent stigma or adverse psychological impact.
